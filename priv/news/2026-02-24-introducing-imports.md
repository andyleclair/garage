One of the most requested features was the ability to import builds from 1977 Garage. Well, the wait is over!

Head on over to [the new build page](/builds/new) to import a build from '77. Please pop by the [Discord](https://discord.gg/3gstJhT298) if you have any issues. I do track when errors occur, but I also love to hear from the users.

I hope you enjoy this new feature, I had fun building it!
